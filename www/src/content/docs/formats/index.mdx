---
title: Shared Formats
description: The bitplane/palette encoding and dungeon/scripting engine shared across the Phantasie trilogy, documented once instead of three times.
---

Not every format in the Phantasie trilogy is game-specific. Two layers are
shared wholesale:

- **[Bitplanes & Palette](/formats/bitplane-and-palette/)** — the pixel
  formula every image format in all three games ultimately reduces to, and
  the 12-bit ST-style palette encoding both the Atari ST-native files and
  the Amiga executables' embedded palettes use.
- **[Dungeon & Scripting Engine](/formats/dungeon-and-scripting-engine/)** —
  Phantasie I and II are read by **one byte-identical parser**: same 33×38
  grid, same cell-ID semantics, same star/action/message trigger format.
  Phantasie III's own variant is close but not byte-identical, and gets its
  own page in its section — cross-linked from here.

Each game's own section documents what's specific to it: container formats
(`.pic`, `.PAT`, `.CMP`/`.CSH`, `.SET`), executable-embedded data tables,
and combat formulas.
