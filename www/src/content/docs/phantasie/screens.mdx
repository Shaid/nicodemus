Phantasie I's 6 full-screen `.pic` pictures, served from
`/assets/phantasie/amiga/`. See [Graphics Formats](../../phantasie/graphics/)
for the raw container format, and its "palette correction" section for the
full story behind why these now render correctly: each file's real
in-game palette (one of 5 slots stored in the `game` executable) was
resolved byte-exact this session via `HUNK_RELOC32` operand tracing rather
than assumed, and the Amiga-native `component*17` colour decode replaced
an Atari-ST-derived lookup that had been silently zeroing 5 of 240
palette-word nibbles project-wide. **These are real decoded pixel data,
not illustrations** — no manual colour correction was applied to any image
below.

## Title sequence (`pal4`, `game+0x194DC`)

Both shown by the same title-sequence function (`game+0x1F042`);
`cover2.pic` blits over `cover1.pic` while title music plays from
`tamb.dat`.
