**Phantasie** (1985, Strategic Simulations, Inc. — "Tholie's Tale") is the
first game of SSI's Phantasie trilogy: a first-person dungeon RPG where a
party of up to six adventurers explores 10 dungeon levels beneath the Isle of
Gelnor, hunting the Black Knights of Nikademus. This is the Amiga original —
the only platform Phantasie I shipped on. It shares one byte-identical
dungeon/scripting engine with Phantasie II (see [Shared
Formats](../formats/)); everything specific to this game — its own raw
graphics containers, executable-embedded data tables, and combat math —
is documented here.

## How the data is organised

The game ships as a 193,804-byte AmigaOS `HUNK` executable (`game`) that
carries the monster/race/item name tables, five 16-colour palettes, and the
`GetStat`/`SetStat` character-record accessor, plus ten paired
`dngN`/`messN` dungeon-and-message files (`N` = 1-10), six raw uncompressed
`.pic` full-screen pictures, and four `.pat` sprite banks (`mstr1`/`mstr2`
for monsters, `party1`/`party2` for playable races). A handful of
non-dungeon world/town files (`twns.int`, `out1.dat`-`out19.dat`, `maps.int`,
`scrolls.dtx`, `tamb.dat`) exist alongside these and are, honestly, mostly
unexplored — see [Status & Open Work](../phantasie/status/).

## What this project did

The goal was to decode every P1-specific container format, name every table
the game reads from its own executable, and trace combat resolution in both
directions. The headline results:

- **All 6 full-screen `.pic` pictures** (`cover1`, `cover2`, `decor`,
  `final`, `logo`, `pelnor`) now render in full, correct colour — each
  file's real in-game palette assignment (one of 5 slots stored in the
  executable) resolved byte-exact via `HUNK_RELOC32` operand tracing, not
  guessed. See [Graphics Formats](../phantasie/graphics/) for the correction
  story behind this.
- **80 monster sprites** across `mstr1.pat`/`mstr2.pat` (normal and
  attacking pose), addressed by two 80-entry big-endian offset/dimension
  tables in the executable — summing every entry's computed byte size
  reproduces `mstr1.pat`'s exact 68,016-byte length with **zero**
  discrepancy.
- **16 playable races** across `party1.pat`/`party2.pat`, fixed 32×32,
  512 bytes/sprite — `16 × 512 = 8,192`, `party1.pat`'s exact size, zero
  remainder.
- **`GetStat`/`SetStat` fully disassembled**: a linear resolver chain
  mapping `statIndex` (1-164) to exact character-record byte offsets.
  12 core stat names plus Score, Armour Rating, Race, Status, and all 8
  skill names are bound to their real on-screen labels via a
  51-instruction, 51/51-byte-verified trace of the character-sheet display
  routine. Two indices, **7 and 8, are structurally confirmed unreachable
  by any display routine in the binary** — a stronger, more specific
  finding than "unknown." See
  [Characters & Combat](../phantasie/characters-and-combat/).
- **Combat resolution traced in both directions.** A linear congruential
  RNG (`seed = (seed*3677+3) mod 32768`) feeds a `RollPercent()` helper;
  hit/miss and damage formulas are fully derived for both a party member
  attacking a monster and a monster attacking a party member — and the two
  directions are **not mirror images**: different roll multipliers (`×1`
  vs. `×6`) and damage divisors (`10` vs. `15`).
- **Item name table**: 181 entries at `game+0x5EFC`, verified by decoding
  every real item-index reference across all 10 real `dng*` files — 63
  distinct indices, **0 out of range**.
- **Two independent oracles agree.** This session's own disassembly is
  cross-checked against SSI's own 1986 *Phantasie I and Phantasie II
  Rulebook* — a genuine primary source found this session and not
  previously used by this project. Its printed character-sheet layout
  independently names Strength, Intelligence, Dexterity, Constitution,
  Charisma, Luck, Age, Hit Points, Magic, Gold in Bank, Exper. Points, and
  Score, and its Skills section names all 8 skills — matching the
  disassembly-derived labels exactly.
