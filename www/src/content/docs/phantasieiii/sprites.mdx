Phantasie III packs its monster and race sprites into 7 grids inside the
fixed 320×200 `.CMP` canvas — 5 banks of 40 frames (32×48 cells, a 10×4
grid) and 2 "giant" banks of 8 frames (80×72 cells, a 4×2 grid), 216 frames
total. See [Graphics Formats](../../phantasieiii/graphics/) for the full
bank/cell layout and the `spriteIndex` dispatcher that assigns monsters to
cells.

**Every bank stores a monster's normal and attacking pose as adjacent
cells** — confirmed by direct disassembly of the bank/cell dispatcher
(`0x1B4F0`-`0x1B6E2`, see Graphics Formats). Each gallery below wires that
confirmed pairing as a hover-swap: hover a card to see the attacking pose.

**Names**: these banks are shared art — Phantasie III's 80 monsters map
onto only 48 distinct (bank, cell) slots across all 7 banks (25 slots are
reused by 2-4 different monsters — a real finding, not a gap; see [Data
Tables](../../phantasieiii/data-tables/) for the exact count and worked
examples). That many-to-one mapping means a single gallery card can't
carry one honest name, so these galleries show the raw cell names rather
than an invented or ambiguous label. The monster ↔ cell correlation itself
*is* documented, with worked examples, on the [Data
Tables](../../phantasieiii/data-tables/) page — it comes from the extraction
pipeline's own `entities.json`, which isn't yet part of this site's asset
mirror.

## Heroes / race bank (`heros.cmp`)

40 frames, 32×48. Bank 0 in the confirmed dispatcher — every monster or
race sprite resolving to bank 0 renders from here. Only one monster maps
here in the confirmed `monsinfo.dat` data (`Troll Baby`, cell 10); the
other 39 cells are the 18 playable races' sprites, addressed through a
confirmed 19-byte lookup table (file `0x299D8`) — **not** race index used
directly as the cell index (6 of 18 races share a cell with another race).
See [Data Tables](../../phantasieiii/data-tables/) for the full
race-to-cell table and the disassembly evidence.
